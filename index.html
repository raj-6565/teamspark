Questions teams ask

Who decides who joins my team?

You do. TeamSpark ranks students and shows why, but nobody is added until the listing owner invites them or accepts their request.

Why not let the system pick the whole team?

A score cannot see how people get along. A suggestion plus a human decision keeps the speed of automation and the judgement of the owner.

Do I need an account?

Not in this prototype. It runs in your browser as a single user. A full version would add sign-in and a shared database.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, so it stays on this device and is not shared with other users.
